01 / ABOUTTHE MISSION

Who are we?

North Texas has no shortage of people working at the intersection of art, design, and technology. There's practitioners, collectives, studios, university programs, and makerspaces. What it's missing isn't talent or activity. It's visibility. S/GNAL seeks to connect those at this intersection. Run by Karina Lujan, a brand designer and creative technologist.

Mission

We connect the people, projects, spaces, and organizations building at the intersection of art, design, and technology across North Texas — making a fragmented creative-tech ecosystem visible, searchable, and easy to join. We start by mapping who's already here. Over time, that map becomes a community, and that community can build the institutions — mentorship, education, exhibitions — it decides it needs.

North Texas

City of Dallas Office of Arts and Culture

Supports, partners, and develops opportunities for Dallas residents, visitors, artists, and related organizations.
1925 Elm Street, STE 400, Dallas, TX
dallasculture.org

Dallas Makerspace

A 36,000 sq ft nonprofit community workshop — 3D fabrication, laser cutting, electronics, blacksmithing, glasswork, and more. Volunteer-run, membership-based.
1825 Monetary Ln, Carrollton, TX
dallasmakerspace.org

Make Space

By the makers of the Mosaic Maker's Collective, Make Space is creating a new third space for creatives of Dallas. It offers workshops, coworking spaces, and studios.
514 N Marsalis Ave, Dallas, TX 75203
make-space.co

Meow Wolf: The Real Unreal

A permanent, maximalist art installation that uses environmental sculptures, sound design, and lighting to tell a story.
3000 Grapevine Mills Pkwy, Suite 253, Grapevine, TX — inside Grapevine Mills mall, between Neiman Marcus and Fieldhouse
meowwolf.com/visit/grapevine

New Media Contemporary

Run by multidisciplinary artist James Talambas, NMC is a gallery, studio, venue, and research space showing new media art, including video, projection, sound, installation, and performance. The space also runs workshops and a weekly live-coding night.
830 Exposition Ave #102, Dallas, TX
newmediacontemporary.com

AURORA (Art, Technology, Community)

Founded in 2010, AURORA is a Dallas nonprofit public arts organization at the intersection of art, tech, and community. Although it has no fixed venue, AURORA puts on biennials and year-round programming.
dallasaurora.com

LIGHTWARE LABS

LIGHTWARE LABS is a project mapping studio founded in Dallas and puts on over 200 events a year.
606 N. Edgefield Ave., Dallas, TX
lightwarelabs.com
